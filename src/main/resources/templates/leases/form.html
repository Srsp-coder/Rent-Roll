Automatic Rent Schedule

When you create a lease, RentRoll automatically generates individual monthly rent payment ledger records covering every month from the Start Date through the End Date.

  • No overlapping leases: The system ensures this property has no existing active lease on the chosen dates.
  • Safe against duplicates: If regenerated, duplicate cycles are strictly avoided.
  • 5% Late-fee engine: Payments that cross the due date automatically incur a 5% late fee upon settlement.
